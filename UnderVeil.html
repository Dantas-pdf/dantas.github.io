UnderVeil

A fast-paced action roguelike dungeon crawler steeped in Greek and Roman mythology.

Genre: Action roguelike Status: In development

What to expect

Descend beneath the veil and fight your way through three mythological dungeons, each with its own enemies, elite guardians, and bosses. Every run is different. Choose your path room by room, weighing which door leads to the reward you need. Rooms shift in shape and layout, so no two fights play out the same. Inspired by Hades, The Binding of Isaac, and Dead Cells.

Fast, responsive combat

Each weapon has its own moveset and feel, and many carry a signature affliction. Set enemies ablaze with Burn, wear them down with Poison, slow them with Frost, tear them open with Bleed, weaken them with Fear, or transform them with a Curse that leaves them helpless.

Shape your build

Collect Omens, divine blessings that can reshape your build and change how a run unfolds. If fate deals you a poor hand, visit the Fates Room and trade your Omens for new ones, provided you can pay the price in blood or gold.

Between runs

Return to the hub between descents and invest in your skill tree to grow stronger for the next run. Play with a keyboard or controller.
